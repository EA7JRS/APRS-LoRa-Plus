Group watch & remote emergency
Watch your group's stations (IN / OUT of range, distance) and let an authorised station start an emergency by APRS message. Both off by default.
Poll pings a station not heard for that time (minimum 60 s). Use long intervals: the channel is shared.
An authorised callsign starts it with the message EAI <code> and ends it with EAI STOP <code>. The code travels unencrypted: it prevents mistakes, it is not security. Cancel with the button gesture. Check that remote control is allowed by your regulations.

IARU region (auto)
Sets the IARU region, channel and frequency of every profile from the GPS position. Off by default.
On request means Detect now here or SYSTEM > IARU: detect now on the tracker screen. It needs a stable GPS fix (about 20 s in the same region; 60 s while moving, so it does not flip at a border). A channel of the band plan becomes the equivalent channel of the new region (or that region's default channel when there is none). A Custom frequency is kept when it is inside the new region's amateur allocation, otherwise it is replaced by the default channel. FT4 / FST4W frequencies outside the new allocation are cleared. WSPR, LR-FHSS, DAPNET and the AIS nautical profile are not touched. While automatic mode is on it overrides the region you set by hand. The limits are the ITU ones, simplified (they can be off by tens of km next to the Region 1 / 3 land borders), and your national rules always prevail.

APRS FSK
APRS FSK 9600 baud (G3RUH). Each profile has its own region, channel, frequency and power. SELECT MODE → APRS FSK; FSK-capable radios only.
CW Beacon
Keyed carrier (A1A), SX1262/SX1268 only. Two radio profiles: CW Beacon and Telegraphy (key, messages, trainer). {CALL} = your callsign without SSID; max 60 characters. SELECT MODE → MORSE.
Yaesu FH-2 keypad (keys 1-5 = CW messages 1-5): ADC pin to GND, 10 kΩ from the pin to 3V3. Pick the pin, save, then press each key and click Read.
Live: -
3.5 mm jack: tip = key / dit, ring = dah, sleeve = GND. Pins are inputs with pull-up (contact to ground); use free pins. The decoder needs the speed above close to yours with a straight key.
Frequency calibration
Oscillator correction in ppm for all modes. Emit a carrier (Tune carrier, WSPR or CW BEACON submenu), measure it with an SDR and enter the error (measured − expected). Positive ppm raises the transmitted frequency. T-TWR: Tune carrier in the RADIO or FM page menu (or the app); the correction applies to the SA868 in transmit and receive (100 Hz steps with the NiceRF firmware, 62.5 Hz with OpenRTX).
The button adds -error / frequency to the ppm field (Hz ÷ MHz = ppm); save afterwards. Measure three times with the tracker warm and the box closed; use the average. WSPR can use this value instead of its own offset.
Satellites
Selected at boot (SELECT MODE → Satellites). Pass prediction (SGP4), azimuth / elevation and Doppler. Orbital elements and frequencies (CelesTrak + SatNOGS) come from the project's daily file: send it from the Android app (SAT tab, Bluetooth) or below (WiFi), choosing the CelesTrak groups. The position comes from the GPS; with no GPS fix the fixed position below is used. LoRa boards listen to CW, FSK (AX.25) and LoRa satellites in 70 cm; the T-TWR follows FM and APRS satellites of its band (2 m or 70 cm) with its FM VFO: Doppler, uplink and CTCSS of the satellite.

Transmit to the satellite (uplink)
Off by default. Only in an amateur band of the radio (LoRa boards: 430–440 MHz; T-TWR: 2 m or 70 cm of the IARU region), above the minimum elevation, only when you ask (menu or app), with Doppler correction. You answer for your licence, the satellite's rules and your power. Needs a real callsign in the active beacon profile. CW sends Morse; FSK an AX.25 UI frame (CALL>APLRT1:text); LoRa the payload. T-TWR: APRS through a digipeater (position or message, path below) and FM voice with the PTT, both with the uplink Doppler and the satellite's CTCSS.
Switching sequencer (preamplifier / PA / external PTT)
Sequence: preamp off and PA / relay on → lead → PTT on → pre → RF → post → PTT off, PA off → tail → preamp on. -1 = not used. The preamp pin is on while listening. Use a transistor / relay driver: the GPIO cannot power a relay or a preamp directly.
Antenna rotor
Satellite mode only. Points the rotor at the selected satellite from pre-position seconds before AOS until LOS. WiFi / TCP: Hamlib rotctld client (port 4533) reachable from the WiFi set in Firmware update. Bluetooth SPP: Yaesu GS-232 (classic Bluetooth boards, tracker Bluetooth off). BLE: the Android app gets the rot event and can relay it. Azimuth 0–359°; 450° rotors not handled.
Azimuth and elevation are computed from the tracker's own GPS position (Satellites 70 cm > Station position). CW beacon: the rotor points to the centre of the locator square sent by the beacon (e.g. VVV DE ED7YAA IM76HR).
Fixed targets (up to 8): repeater, beacon, station… The Android app can fill the list by Bluetooth (tgt). Decimal degrees (east +), altitude in metres. A slot with no name is empty.

VHF radio (SA868)
LilyGO T-TWR Plus: analogue APRS, AFSK 1200 baud. The channel list follows the IARU region (and the automatic IARU region, when on). Rev2.1 cannot change the power. Set the TX level on a receiver for about 2.5 kHz deviation (Narrow) or 3 kHz (Wide). SELECT MODE → APRS FSK 9600 uses G3RUH 9600 baud: the module's voice filters are bypassed and the channel is 25 kHz (needs a 9600-capable station at the other end; best with the OpenRTX module firmware). In 9600 a UHF module uses the FSK frequency and protocol of the active LoRa profile, like the other boards. Reception always takes AX.25, FX.25 and IL2P. SELECT MODE → M17: digital M17 (4FSK, 25 kHz, flat audio) on the M17 frequency; APRS goes out as M17 APRS packets (other T-TWRs in M17 mode read them), and the tracker hears M17 SMS and the callsigns and GNSS positions of M17 voice calls (shown as stations; never sent to APRS-IS). Default M17 frequency: 144.875 / 433.475 MHz (IARU Region 1 suggestion).
MORSE mode (CW in VHF / UHF)
Frequencies and power: CW section (outside the band of the module: 144.050 / 432.050 MHz). A1A keys the module (NiceRF: PTT, up to ~15-18 wpm; OpenRTX: one register write per edge, up to ~12 wpm). Reception detects the carrier by the quieting of the FM noise; on Rev2.1 the speaker gives a 700 Hz tone.
M17 mode
Transmits only in the amateur bands of the IARU region where it is, by the GPS position: Europe (R1) 144–146 and 430–440 MHz, Americas (R2) 144–148 and 430–450 MHz, R3 144–148 and 430–440 MHz; without a position, 144–146 and 430–440 MHz. Receives 136–174 / 400–480 MHz. These limits are fixed in the firmware.
FM voice (T-TWR)
SELECT MODE → FM voice: the T-TWR as a 2 m / 70 cm handheld. Side PTT with the board microphone, the encoder tunes on the FM page (long press: menu). ARS sets the repeater shift in repeater segments. Squelch Auto: on Rev2.1 the ESP32 feeds the speaker and closes on noise (with noise reduction if chosen); on Rev2.0 it adjusts the module level. ARTS polls with the DCS code every 15 / 25 s (IN / OUT RANGE, like Yaesu); EAI transmits beeps every 2.5 s or the open microphone, with the callsign in CW, for the EAI time (also after the man-down time without touching the radio). APRS beacons go out on the APRS frequency and come back; with Frequency in beacon the comment starts with the channel ("145.650MHz T088 -060") so other APRS radios can QSY. On the LAST APRS page, QSY (FM voice) tunes a station that announces a frequency.

AIS receiver (marine VHF)
Vessels on 161.975 / 162.025 MHz. T-TWR (VHF module): SELECT MODE → AIS receiver (receive only, flat audio). Any board: an external AIS module with NMEA output (dAISy, Quark-elec...) on UART 2 (shared with the NMEA output if that is on). The Android app can also feed it from its RTL-SDR. Page AIS (list, details, GO TO), collision alarm (CPA / TCPA, guard zone), AIS-SART / MOB / EPIRB alarm, and the !AIVDM sentences to the NMEA output and the app.
Broadcast radio (I2C)
FM broadcast receiver on the I2C bus of the display: RDA5807M (no reset pin), Si4703 (needs the reset pin) or Si4735 (reset pin; also MW / SW / LW). Detected at start-up; the audio comes out of the module's own jack, the tracker controls it. Page BCAST (long press: seek, volume, band, presets), app and BLE bcast. Presets: MHz for FM (101.5) or kHz for AM (999).
DAPNET Pager
SELECT MODE → DAPNET. Receive-only POCSAG pager (never transmits): messages for your RICs and the time RICs go to the last-messages list. SX1262/SX1268 boards only.
Default 439.9875 MHz is the usual DAPNET frequency. A RIC is the pager address (0–2097151). Not verified on air yet.
WSPR Beacon
SELECT MODE → WSPR. Type 1 message: callsign without SSID (6 characters max), 4-character locator, power. Needs a GPS fix. Fixed 70 cm frequency: dial 432.300 MHz USB, centre 432301500 Hz; the oscillator must be within ±100 Hz (measure with an SDR, Tune carrier). Use a low-pass filter and your licence.

FT8 / FT4 / FST4W Beacon
SELECT MODE → FT8 / FT4 / FST4W beacon. Transmit only. FT8 / FT4 send "CQ CALL LOCATOR" or your text; FST4W sends callsign, locator and power. Slots from GPS time. The frequency is the DIAL frequency (tone 0 is 1500 Hz above it, as in WSJT-X). 70 cm only; follow your band plan.
The global ppm correction is applied on top of the extra offset. FT8 on 70 cm: 432.174 MHz (WSJT-X); FT4 and FST4W have no default and do not transmit until a dial frequency is set. FST4W power rounds to 0, 3, 7, 10, 13, 17 or 20 dBm. FST4W-300 is produced by alternating two frequency steps: try FST4W-120 first. Not tested on hardware yet.
LR-FHSS Beacon experimental
Experimental, transmit only (SX1262/SX1268). Off until ticked; then it appears in SELECT MODE. Sends "PB1 CALL LOCATOR6 dBm" every N minutes. You need the authorisation your national rules require.
Default 434.000 MHz, 39 kHz grid, rate 1/3 (inside the IARU R1 125 kHz data-like-LoRa note). Frequencies overlapping 432.000-432.500 or 435-438 MHz are refused. Experimental.

KISS over WiFi
KISS TNC over WiFi for APRSDroid, Xastir, YAAC… (APRS LoRa / FSK; ESP32-S3 / C3).
Joins the WiFi set in Administration > Firmware update and keeps it on (more battery, no deep sleep). Address shown on screen; also aprs-lora-plus.local. No password: anyone allowed here can transmit with your callsign. An entry ending in a dot (192.168.1.) allows that whole network.
CAT (Hamlib) over WiFi / USB
Lets WSJT-X, fldigi, GPredict… and any Hamlib program talk to the tracker as a radio (Hamlib NET rigctl, model 2). Over WiFi (same network as KISS; ESP32-S3 / C3 boards) or over the USB serial port. APRS LoRa and APRS FSK modes.
Hamlib: rigctl -m 2 -r aprs-lora-plus.local:4532 (WiFi) or the USB backend in tools/hamlib_backend (115200 baud, DTR and RTS off). No password: anyone allowed here can retune the radio and, with PTT enabled, make it transmit APRS packets (never audio). See docs/CAT_HAMLIB.md.
NMEA output (TTL → RS422)
Serial NMEA 0183 for a plotter via a TTL→RS422 converter. T-Beam S3 Supreme only.
Applied after saving and restarting. Sends AIS !AIVDM and MDA. Wiring: TX pin → converter DI/TXD, VCC → 3V3 (DC1), GND → GND, A/B (Y/Z) → plotter Rx+ / Rx−. Suggested pin: 43 (TXD).
